# How to Optimize Your Resume for ATS

If you are applying to jobs online, your resume is almost certainly being read by a robot before it ever reaches a human. These robots are called **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)**.

## What is an ATS?

An ATS is a software application that enables the electronic handling of recruitment and hiring needs. Companies like Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever use these systems to filter out up to 75% of applicants automatically.

## The Secret: Job Description (JD) Matching

To beat the ATS, you must tailor your resume to the specific job description.

1. **Use Exact Keywords:** If the JD asks for "Project Management", do not write "Managed Projects". Use the exact phrase.
2. **Avoid Complex Formatting:** Tables, columns, and graphics can confuse the ATS parser. Keep it simple.
3. **Use Standard Headings:** Use "Work Experience" instead of "My Professional Journey".
